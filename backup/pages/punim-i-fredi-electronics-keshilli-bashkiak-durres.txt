Advanced Conference and Voting System Installation by Fredi Electronics at the Municipality of Durrës
Project Description:
Fredi Electronics successfully implemented a comprehensive conference and voting system at the Municipality of Durrës, integrating state-of-the-art audio solutions, intelligent voting systems, and advanced recording & streaming software. The project also included the deployment of a real-time voting result display, ensuring seamless and transparent communication during council sessions.
This installation enhances the efficiency of municipal operations by providing a modern, fully integrated, and user-friendly conference environment, supporting digital governance and public transparency.
