Ministria e Arsimit-Mikrofona Konference :
Fredi Electronics has successfully completed the design and implementation of a fully integrated smart audio-video conference system at the Ministry of Education. This project marks another step forward in the modernization of institutional infrastructure through cutting-edge technology.
The installed system includes:
WiFi conference microphones with high-quality audio transmission, push-to-talk functionality, interference protection, and long-lasting rechargeable batteries—completely wireless for clean and flexible use.
Central control unit (intelligent server) that manages:
Speaker priority settings;
Automatic digital recording of every session;
Integration with online platforms (Zoom, Teams, YouTube, etc.) for live streaming.
Professional wall-mounted speaker system for uniform and high-quality sound coverage across the room.
