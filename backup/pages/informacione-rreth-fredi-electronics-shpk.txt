Frequently Asked Questions
Does Fredi Electronics offer installation and after-sales support?
Yes. Our technical team provides installation, calibration, and maintenance services for all products we supply. We also offer on-site support and periodic servicing to ensure systems continue performing at their best.
Where des Fredi Electronics operate?
Fredi Electronics is based in Tirana, Albania , and operates across the entire Albanian market as well as Kosovo . We also support regional projects through our network of partners and resellers.
What is the warranty on your products?
Most of our products include a minimum 12-month warranty , depending on the manufacturer and product category. Extended warranties and service contracts are available for large installations or long-term projects.
How can I request a quote or consultation?
You can contact us directly at fredielectronics@gmail.com , by phone at +355 69 67 52 032 , or through the Contact page on our website. Our team will review your requirements and provide a tailored quotation or project proposal.
If I own an environment and need an audio system but don’t know what equipment is required, can Fredi help?
Absolutely. Our specialists can visit your location and perform an acoustic assessment to understand the space, sound requirements, and layout. Based on that, we design a complete audio solution – including speaker placement, amplification, and control systems – tailored to your environment.

Fredi Electronics sh.p.k. is one of the leading and largest distributors of electronic solutions in Albania. Established in 1991 to meet the growing demand for high-quality electronic products in the local market, the company has since expanded and solidified its presence, covering the entire Albanian market as well as extending its operations to Kosovo.
At Fredi Electronics , customers have access to a diverse portfolio of over 8,700 product models across multiple categories, offering innovative, high-quality, and reliable solutions tailored to various industry needs.
Our Product Categories:
Audio & Video Solutions – A premium selection of amplifiers, microphones, mixers, and audio accessories, delivering exceptional sound quality and performance for both professional and commercial applications.
Security Systems – We provide advanced security solutions to safeguard your premises, including access control devices, surveillance cameras, alarm systems, and recording equipment for comprehensive protection.
LED Display Panels – We offer a wide range of LED panels in various sizes, resolutions, and configurations , ensuring high brightness, energy efficiency, and vibrant visuals for indoor and outdoor applications.
Lighting Systems – Our lighting solutions cover indoor and outdoor LED technology , including LED strips, moving head lights, stage lighting, and architectural illumination , designed to enhance any space with efficiency and style.
Fire Protection Systems – With  installations all across the Balkans , our fire protection solutions are designed for maximum safety and efficiency , undergoing routine inspections to ensure compliance with the highest standards.
IT & Networking Solutions – We provide cutting-edge IT and networking technologies , ensuring seamless connectivity and robust infrastructure for businesses and organizations.
Smart Home Technology – Our portfolio includes state-of-the-art smart home solutions , integrating automation, security, and energy-efficient devices to enhance modern living.
